Full-Body 2x — Strength + Polarized Cardio

Version 1.0.0. Model time limit for each lifting session: 60 minutes.

Two full-body strength sessions (Tuesday/Sunday) combined with polarized cycling cardio (Friday VO2 Max 5x3 + Mon/Wed/Sat Zone 2).

BLUF: Session times are model estimates for planning.
The time includes warmup sets, both sides, work sets, rest and station changes.
It does not include the general warmup, travel or cardio.
The ±20% planning range is an allowance, not a measured confidence interval.
If you finish early, it is not necessary to add sets.

BLUF: For “per side” sets, do both sides.
Record the two sides as one set.
Warmup reps are also per side.
Do not go above the target RPE.

General rules

The exact exercises, sets, reps, RPE, warmup ramps, rests and cardio doses are coaching choices. Grgic 2022 supports non-failure training; accessories need not reach failure. Currier 2026 concerns healthy-adult resistance training, not exercise-specific injury protection. The 48-hour buffer is a scheduling choice, not a recovery guarantee.
